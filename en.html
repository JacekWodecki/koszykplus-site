FAQ

Short and clear.

Do I need an account to use Basket+?

No. Basket+ requires no account or sign-in. Sync and sharing use iCloud when it is enabled on your device.

Are shared lists a paid feature?

No. Standard list sharing and conversation on a shared list are included in Basket+ Free.

What exactly is included in Basket+ Plus?

Plus unlocks events, receipts, reports and expense settlements, and removes ads for the subscriber.

How is my data synced?

Through iCloud and CloudKit. Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud account; we do not operate our own user-account database.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You manage the subscription in your Apple account settings. After cancellation, Plus remains active until the end of the paid period.

Where can I get help?

Email hello@basketplus.app or visit our support page.
