Ads in Free

  • Discreet native Google AdMob ads appear in two places: on the home screen and in Spending. They are not shown on shopping lists or in chat.
  • Ad personalisation is switched off. That does not mean Google uses no identifiers – it may use them, for example, for measurement and frequency capping.
  • Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), ads do not load without your consent. iPhone may also ask for tracking permission; declining does not block the app.
  • With Plus the ads disappear and the app stops requesting them.

iCloud and sharing

No Basket+ account.
With iCloud, when you want it.

What works without iCloud and what needs it.

No Basket+ account

You do not create an account or sign in. We do not run our own user-account database.

With iCloud

When you are signed in to iCloud, lists sync automatically between your devices and sharing relies on Apple invitations. Your data lives in your iCloud account.

Without iCloud

Without an iCloud account, or when Basket+ is switched off in iCloud settings, the app works locally on the device – without sync and sharing.

Sharing requirements

Both sides need iCloud and Basket+; the invitation is accepted in the system Apple dialog. A person on Free can accept a standard list; an event requires their own Plus.

FAQ

Short and clear.

Do I need an account to use Basket+?

No. Basket+ requires no account or sign-in. Sync and sharing use iCloud when you are signed in to it.

Does the app work without iCloud?

Yes, locally on your device. Without iCloud there is no sync between devices and no sharing of lists with other people.

Are shared lists a paid feature?

No. Standard list sharing and conversation on a shared list are included in Basket+ Free. Events require Plus.

Who can join a shared list or an event?

A standard list: anyone with Basket+ and iCloud, including on Free. An event: only people with their own active Plus; someone else’s Plus unlocks nothing for you.

What exactly is included in Basket+ Plus?

Plus unlocks events, receipts, reports and expense settlements, and removes ads for the subscriber.

What ads are shown in Free?

Discreet native Google AdMob ads on the home screen and in Spending, without personalisation. Where the law requires it, only after your consent. Plus removes them. Details: Privacy Policy.

How much is Plus, and is there a trial?

You will see the price and any trial period in the app and on the App Store before you buy anything. We do not list them on this website.

How is my data synced?

Through iCloud and CloudKit. Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud account; we do not operate our own user-account database.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You manage the subscription in your Apple account settings. After cancellation, Plus remains active until the end of the paid period.

Where can I get help?

Email hello@basketplus.app or visit our support page.
